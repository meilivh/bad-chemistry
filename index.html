With all the free allowances the European Union’s chemical sector receive under the EU’s Emissions Trading System, it effectively pays no carbon price for its pollution, which is grossly unfair and counterproductive.

From 2013 to 2024, European chemical installations emitted 1.28 GtCO₂e.

only 2% of it came at a cost

the rest was covered by free allowances.

The chemical sector has pumped 1.3 gigatonnes of CO₂e into the atmosphere since 2013

That makes it the EU ETS's 4th largest industrial emitter.

Emissions have mostly remained stable since 2013

Emissions reductions are most likely due to installation closures since 2014.

The sector has barely paid for its pollution

Since 2013, the sector received 1.26 billion free allowances, covering 98% of its total emissions.
